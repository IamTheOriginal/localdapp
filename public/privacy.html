Privacy

Non-commercial, local-first peer-to-peer deployment.

1. Your data stays on your devices

Dapp Builder is local-first: apps, records, settings, and cryptographic keys live in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). There are no user accounts, no central app database, no tracking cookies, and no analytics scripts.

2. Peer-to-peer sync & signaling

When devices sync a shared dapp, they connect directly via encrypted WebRTC channels. A stateless signaling broker relays only the initial WebRTC handshake (SDP offers/answers and opaque room IDs) in memory without storing app content. Public STUN servers (or your own configured TURN/ICE relay) may be used for NAT traversal; only connection metadata (such as IP addresses) is processed during connection setup.

3. Deleting your data

Because all app data resides exclusively on your device, clearing site data in your browser deletes everything locally. You can also configure your own signaling and TURN servers in Settings → Signaling.
